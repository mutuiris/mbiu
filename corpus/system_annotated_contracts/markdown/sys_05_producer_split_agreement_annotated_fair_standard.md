## CLAUSE 3: PRODUCER MASTER ROYALTY AND 50/50 SONGWRITING SPLIT SHEET

**3.1 Producer Royalty (4% of Gross After Recoupment).** In addition to the KES 60,000 Production Fee (which shall be treated as a recoupable advance against Producer royalties), the Artist shall pay (or direct the Artist's digital distributor via a Letter of Direction to pay) to the Producer a royalty of **four percent (4%) of Gross Receipts** (with the Artist retaining ninety-six percent (96%) of Gross Receipts) received from digital streaming, downloads, and Skiza Tunes exploitation of the Master, payable semi-annually within 60 days after 30th June and 31st December once the KES 60,000 Production Fee has been recouped.

**3.2 50/50 Musical Composition (Publishing) Split Sheet.** Because the Artist wrote the lyrics and vocal melody and the Producer composed the original instrumental music, the copyright and publishing income in the underlying musical composition of *"Jua Kali"* shall be co-owned equally as a work of joint authorship under the Copyright Act (Cap. 130): **fifty percent (50%) by the Artist** and **fifty percent (50%) by the Producer**. Each Party may register its respective 50% share directly with KECOBO and MCSK, and neither Party shall grant a commercial brand synchronization license for the Track without the other Party's written consent (not to be unreasonably withheld).

## CLAUSE 4: MORAL RIGHTS CREDIT, SAMPLE WARRANTY AND MUTUAL AUDIT

**4.1 Producer Credit and Moral Rights.** Pursuant to Section 32 of the Copyright Act (Cap. 130), both the Artist and the Producer retain their Moral Rights. The Artist shall credit the Producer as *"Produced by Kazi Beats"* in all DSP metadata and YouTube descriptions.

**4.2 No Uncleared Samples Warranty & Mutual Audit.** The Producer warrants that the instrumental beat is one hundred percent (100%) original and contains **no uncleared third-party audio samples or loops**, and shall indemnify the Artist against any copyright takedown or third-party claim if an uncleared sample was used by the Producer. Each Party shall have the right once per year upon 30 days written notice to inspect the other Party's royalty statements relating to the Track, and either Party may terminate any breached license upon 30 days written notice if a material breach or non-payment is not cured. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Kenya.
