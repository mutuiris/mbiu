## CLAUSE 1: PERFORMANCE FEE AND PRE-STAGE SETTLEMENT

**1.1 Gross Fee (50% Deposit / 50% Prior to Stage Walk-On).** The Promoter shall pay the Artist a fixed Performance Fee of **Kenya Shillings Two Hundred and Fifty Thousand (KES 250,000)** on a gross receipts basis (subject only to 5% resident Withholding Tax under the Income Tax Act, Cap. 470), payable in two installments: (a) a non-refundable booking deposit of 50% (**KES 125,000**) upon execution of this Agreement; and (b) the remaining balance of 50% (**KES 125,000**) via real-time bank transfer or M-Pesa at least **2 hours prior to the Artist's scheduled stage walk-on time** on 31st December, 2026. If the 100% full fee is not settled prior to stage walk-on, the Artist may decline to perform while retaining the 50% deposit and maintaining her right to the full balance.

## CLAUSE 4: SOUNDCHECK, TECHNICAL SAFETY AND CANCELLATION ALLOCATION

**4.1 Technical Rider, Stage Safety and Cancellation Protection.** The Promoter shall provide professional stage, lighting, and sound equipment in accordance with the Artist's Technical Rider and shall provide a 60 minutes soundcheck between 14:00 and 17:00 EAT on the Event Date, plus 24 hours backstage security. If the Promoter cancels the Event within 14 days of the Event Date for any reason other than Force Majeure, 100% of the KES 250,000 Performance Fee shall be payable to the Artist. If the Event is prevented by Force Majeure (such as extreme weather or government order), the Artist shall retain the 50% Booking Deposit (KES 125,000) and the Parties shall in good faith negotiate a rescheduled date. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Kenya.
