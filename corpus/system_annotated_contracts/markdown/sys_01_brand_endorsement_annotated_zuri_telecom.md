## CLAUSE 1: CAMPAIGN DELIVERABLES AND CREATIVE APPROVAL

**1.1 Deliverables and Scheduling.** During the Term, the Artist shall provide two (2) commercial filming days in Nairobi, three (3) live stage appearances at Zuri 5G campus concerts, and six (6) social media posts on Instagram and TikTok. The Brand shall give the Artist at least 10 days (ten days) prior written notice of each scheduled date and shall pay all travel, security, styling, and production costs directly without deducting them from the Artist's fee.

> ### [EXPERT LEGAL & MBIU SYSTEM ANNOTATION — CLAUSE 1.1]
> - **Clause ID:** `1.1` | **Topic:** `boilerplate` (Scope of Services & Production Costs) | **Affects:** `money`
> - **Drafted For:** `artist` | **Comparison Outcome:** `matches_standard` | **Mbiu Label:** `typical` | **Implication:** `none`
> - **Reference Standard Benchmark (`KECOBO-SYNC-STD-01`):** *"All studio, filming, styling, transport, and production expenses required by the Sponsor shall be paid directly by the Sponsor and shall not be recoupable from the Artist's fee, with appearance dates scheduled upon at least 10 days prior written notice."*
> - **Analysis:**
>   1. **Notice Period Protection:** A 10-day advance written notice requirement protects the Artist from "on-call" ambush scheduling that forces cancellation of independent club or concert bookings.
>   2. **Non-Recoupable Production Costs:** Explicitly stating that the Brand pays travel, security, styling, and production costs *without deducting them from the Artist's fee* prevents one of the most common Kenyan brand-deal traps where agency production budgets are quietly subtracted from the headline endorsement fee.

## CLAUSE 2: TERM AND TERRITORY

**2.1 Six-Month Campaign Term.** Subject to the archival and digital provisions of Clause 4.3, the term of this Agreement shall be 6 months (six months) commencing on the Effective Date and ending on 31st May, 2027 (the **"Term"**), within the territory of the Republic of Kenya (the **"Territory"**).

> ### [EXPERT LEGAL & MBIU SYSTEM ANNOTATION — CLAUSE 2.1]
> - **Clause ID:** `2.1` | **Topic:** `term_duration` | **Affects:** `time`
> - **Drafted For:** `label` | **Comparison Outcome:** `worse_for_you` | **Mbiu Label:** `worth_asking_about` | **Implication:** `major`
> - **Linked Clauses:** `["4.3"]` (`override`: `"Subject to the archival and digital provisions of Clause 4.3"`)
> - **Reference Standard Benchmark (`KECOBO-SYNC-STD-01`):** *"The license and endorsement term shall be for a fixed duration of 6 months within the Republic of Kenya, upon the expiry of which all commercial broadcast and digital usage of the Artist's music and likeness shall cease."*
> - **Analysis:**
>   1. **Cross-Clause Subordination Trap:** On its face, Clause 2.1 looks like a clean 6-month Kenyan term. However, the opening carve-out (*"Subject to the archival and digital provisions of Clause 4.3"*) subordinates the 6-month limit to Clause 4.3, which grants the Brand a **perpetual worldwide license** on digital and social platforms.
>   2. **Why Mbiu Maps the Whole Contract First:** If evaluated in isolation, Clause 2.1 would falsely appear to match the 6-month standard. Whole-contract mapping links Clause 2.1 and Clause 4.3 so the artist is warned upfront that the 6-month limit does not apply to online ads.

## CLAUSE 4: COPYRIGHT OWNERSHIP AND SYNCHRONIZATION LICENSE

**4.1 Artist Retains Song Ownership.** Pursuant to the Copyright Act (Cap. 130, Laws of Kenya), the Artist retains one hundred percent (100%) ownership of the copyright in the musical composition and Master Sound Recording of the Licensed Track (*"Usiku wa Nairobi"*), and retains the unrestricted right to collect broadcast and public performance royalties through MCSK, KAMP, and PRISK.

**4.2 Television and Radio Synchronization License.** The Artist grants the Brand a non-exclusive synchronization and master use license to use the Licensed Track in television and radio commercials strictly within the Territory during the 6 months Term.

**4.3 Perpetual Worldwide Digital & Adaptation Override.** **Notwithstanding Clause 2.1, Clause 4.1, or Clause 4.2**, the Artist hereby grants to the Brand and its corporate affiliates an **irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide license in perpetuity** to synchronize, remix, translate into local dialects, and stream the Licensed Track and the Artist's likeness across all internet, social media, YouTube, mobile app, and digital platforms, and waives all Moral Rights under Section 32 of the Copyright Act (Cap. 130) in respect of such digital adaptations.
