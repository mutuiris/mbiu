## CLAUSE 1: EXCLUSIVE TERM AND ALBUM-CYCLE OPTIONS

**1.1 Initial Period and Three Unilateral Album Options.** The Artist engages to render exclusive recording services to the Company throughout the Universe for an Initial Contract Period commencing on the Effective Date and continuing until 12 months after the commercial release by the Company of one (1) complete ten-track (10-track) Album, plus **three (3) consecutive unilateral Option Periods** of one (1) Album each (four (4) Albums in total), exercisable at the Company's sole discretion. No Contract Period shall expire until 12 months after the Company releases the Album for that Period, and the Company shall be under no obligation to release any Album within a fixed calendar date.

> ### [EXPERT LEGAL & MBIU SYSTEM ANNOTATION — CLAUSE 1.1]
> - **Clause ID:** `1.1` | **Topic:** `term_duration` | **Affects:** `time`
> - **Drafted For:** `label` | **Comparison Outcome:** `worse_for_you` | **Mbiu Label:** `worth_asking_about` | **Implication:** `major`
> - **Reference Standard Benchmark (`KECOBO-REC-STD-01`):** *"The Initial Period shall be for a fixed duration of 12 months (capped at 18 months maximum), with at most one (1) mutually agreed Option Period of 12 months, and if the Company fails to commercially release the delivered Album within 6 months of delivery, the Artist may terminate the Term."*
> - **Legal Expert Analysis:**
>   1. **Open-Ended "Delivery + Release" Term Trap:** Because each Contract Period runs until *12 months after commercial release* AND the Company explicitly disclaims any obligation to release an Album by a fixed date, the Company can "shelf" an Album and freeze the Artist's career indefinitely across 4 Albums (1 Initial + 3 Options).
>   2. **Absence of a Release Commitment ("Stop-Clock"):** Standard KECOBO and WIPO templates always pair delivery commitments with a mandatory 6 months release deadline and an 18 months calendar cap.
> - **Mbiu Verified Card Fields:**
>   - `headline`: `"4-album lock-in with no release deadline"`

>   - `what_it_says`: `"You are tied to the Company for 1 Initial Album plus 3 Option Albums (4 Albums total). Each period only ends 12 months after the Company chooses to release the Album."`
>   - `what_it_means`: `"Because the Company has no deadline to release your Album, your contract term could stretch on for many years while you are blocked from recording for anyone else."`
>   - `comparison_note`: `"Standard KECOBO recording terms set a fixed 12 months Initial Period (capped at 18 months) with at most 1 option and a 6 months release deadline, whereas your deal has 3 options."`
>   - `fairer_wording`: `"The Initial Period shall be for a fixed duration of 12 months (capped at 18 months maximum), with at most one (1) mutually agreed Option Period of 12 months, and if the Company fails to commercially release the delivered Album within 6 months of delivery, the Artist may terminate the Term."`

## CLAUSE 2: PERPETUAL ASSIGNMENT OF MASTER RIGHTS AND MORAL RIGHTS WAIVER

**2.1 Work Made for Hire and Perpetual Assignment.** All Master Sound Recordings recorded by the Artist during the Term shall be a "Work Made for Hire" for the Company. The Artist hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company **in perpetuity throughout the Universe** one hundred percent (100%) of all copyright and producer rights in the Masters under Sections 22, 28, and 33 of the Copyright Act (Cap. 130, Laws of Kenya), with no right of reversion to the Artist at any time, and irrevocably waives all Moral Rights under Section 32 of the Copyright Act (Cap. 130).

> ### [EXPERT LEGAL & MBIU SYSTEM ANNOTATION — CLAUSE 2.1]
> - **Clause ID:** `2.1` | **Topic:** `grant_of_rights` | **Affects:** `ownership`
> - **Drafted For:** `label` | **Comparison Outcome:** `worse_for_you` | **Mbiu Label:** `worth_asking_about` | **Implication:** `major`
> - **Reference Standard Benchmark (`KECOBO-REC-STD-01` & `WIPO-REC-STD-02`):** *"The Artist grants the Company an exclusive license to exploit the Master Sound Recordings for a fixed license period of 7 years from release, or until 5 years after full recoupment of recording costs, whereupon all master rights shall revert to the Artist, and the Artist's moral rights under Section 32 of the Copyright Act (Cap. 130) are preserved."*
> - **Legal Expert Analysis:**
>   1. **Permanent Loss of Catalogue (`in perpetuity` + No Reversion):** Under Section 33 of the Kenya Copyright Act (Cap. 130), a written assignment transfers economic ownership permanently unless a reversion clause or license period is specified. Even after the Company recoups every shilling of its recording costs, the Company owns the Artist's Masters forever.

## CLAUSE 4: ROYALTY RATES AND NET RECEIPTS DEDUCTIONS

**4.1 Royalty Rate and Deductions.** Subject to Clause 7.2, the Company shall credit the Artist's royalty account with a base royalty of **ten percent (10%) of Net Receipts** after deducting: (a) a twenty percent (20%) packaging and digital container charge; (b) a fifteen percent (15%) free goods allowance; and (c) third-party distributor fees. On all digital streaming (including Spotify, Boomplay, Apple Music, and Mdundo) and Safaricom Skiza Tunes, the Artist's royalty rate shall be reduced by fifty percent (50%) to **five percent (5%) of Net Receipts**, and the Company may hold thirty percent (30%) of royalties as reserves for four (4) semi-annual accounting periods.
