## CLAUSE 1: APPOINTMENT, TERM AND AUTOMATIC RENEWAL

**1.1 Five-Year Initial Term with Unilateral Renewal.** The Artist appoints the Manager as the Artist's sole and exclusive Personal Manager throughout the world for an Initial Term of **5 years (five years)** from the Effective Date, plus one (1) unilateral Option Period of **3 years (three years)** (8 years in total) exercisable by the Manager in its sole discretion without any minimum income threshold. The Manager may assign its duties to any employee or sub-agent, and no specific individual's departure from the Manager shall entitle the Artist to terminate this Agreement.

## CLAUSE 2: MANAGEMENT COMMISSION ON GROSS RECEIPTS (INCLUDING RECORDING & TOUR BUDGETS)

**2.1 25% Commission on All Gross Receipts Without Deductions.** As compensation for its services, the Manager shall be entitled to receive **twenty-five percent (25%) of one hundred percent (100%) of the Artist's Gross Receipts** from all entertainment, music, live performance, songwriting, and brand endorsement activities, calculated **prior to the deduction of any expenses whatsoever**, and expressly **including** any recording budgets, music video production grants, sound and lighting hire, backing band wages, and tour support advances received by or on behalf of the Artist.

**2.2 Perpetual Post-Term Sunset Commission.** Following the expiration or termination of this Agreement for any reason, the Manager shall continue to receive its full **twenty-five percent (25%) commission in perpetuity** on all gross income generated from any recording, song, album, endorsement, or contract created, negotiated, or touched upon during the Term.

## CLAUSE 3: UNCAPPED EXPENSES, COLLECTION OF FUNDS AND POWER OF ATTORNEY

**3.1 Uncapped Expense Deduction Without Prior Approval.** In addition to its 25% commission, the Manager may incur travel, hospitality, PR, legal, and administrative expenses in its sole discretion without any monthly monetary cap or prior written consent from the Artist, and may reimburse itself one hundred percent (100%) of such expenses directly out of the Artist's Gross Receipts.

**3.2 Exclusive Collection into Manager's General Bank Account & Power of Attorney.** The Manager is hereby granted an irrevocable Power of Attorney to sign all live performance, endorsement, and recording contracts in the Artist's name without prior consultation, and to collect one hundred percent (100%) of the Artist's earnings directly into the Manager's general operating bank account. The Manager shall remit net balances to the Artist once per year (1 year) within 90 days after 31st December, with no right of audit by the Artist.
