## CLAUSE 1: FULL COPYRIGHT ASSIGNMENT OF PAST AND FUTURE COMPOSITIONS

**1.1 100% Assignment of All Compositions in Perpetuity.** The Writer hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Publisher **one hundred percent (100%) of the entire copyright and publishing rights** (including all economic rights under Section 26 of the Copyright Act, Cap. 130, Laws of Kenya) in and to all musical compositions written or co-written by the Writer prior to or during the Term (the **"Compositions"**), together with the sole and exclusive right to administer the Compositions **in perpetuity throughout the Universe**.

> - **Reference Standard Benchmark (`KECOBO-PUB-STD-01` & `WIPO-PUB-STD-03`):** *"Under a publishing administration standard, the Writer retains 100% of the copyright in the Compositions and grants the Publisher an administration license for a fixed Term of 3 years; or under a co-publishing standard, the Writer assigns at most 50% of the Publisher's Share (25% of total copyright) subject to reversion upon expiry of the Retention Period."*
> - **Legal Expert Analysis:**
>   1. **Old-School 100% Publishing Grab vs. Modern Admin / Co-Pub:** Assigning 100% of the entire copyright *in perpetuity*—including both the Writer's entire back catalogue and all new songs—strips the Writer of ownership forever. Modern KECOBO and WIPO standards strongly favour a **Publishing Administration** model (Writer keeps 100% copyright; Publisher takes a 15%–20% commission for 3 years) or at worst a **50/50 Co-Publishing** split with reversion.

## CLAUSE 2: TERM AND MINIMUM SONG DELIVERY QUOTA

**2.1 Three-Year Initial Term Plus Two Options with 15-Song Annual Quota.** The Term of this Agreement shall consist of an Initial Period of 12 months plus two (2) unilateral Option Periods of 12 months each (3 years in total), exercisable by the Publisher. During each 12 months Period, the Writer shall write and deliver at least **fifteen (15) wholly original, commercially satisfactory 100% Compositions**. If the Writer delivers a co-written song (e.g., 50% split with a producer), it shall count as only half (0.5) of a Composition toward the 15-song quota (requiring 30 co-written songs per year), and the Period shall automatically extend without additional advance until the full quota is satisfied.

## CLAUSE 3: PUBLISHING ADVANCE AND ROYALTIES ("RECEIPTS IN KENYA" BASIS)

**3.1 Recoupable Advance.** The Publisher shall pay the Writer a recoupable signing advance of **Kenya Shillings One Hundred and Fifty Thousand (KES 150,000)** for the Initial Period.

**3.2 Royalties Calculated on "Net Receipts in Kenya" After Foreign Sub-Publisher Cuts.** Subject to full recoupment of the Advance and Clause 5.2, the Publisher shall credit the Writer with **fifty percent (50%) of Net Receipts** actually received by the Publisher in Kenya, after first deducting: (a) a twenty percent (20%) Publisher administration fee off the top; (b) up to fifty percent (50%) retained by foreign or regional sub-publishers outside Kenya; and (c) all registration, legal, demo, and collection expenses.

## CLAUSE 5: REVERSION OF UNEXPLOITED SONGS AND CROSS-CLAUSE OVERRIDE

**5.1 Nominal Reversion of Unexploited Compositions.** Subject to Clause 5.2 below, if the Publisher fails to secure a commercial recording release or synchronization placement for an unrecorded Composition within 36 months after delivery, the Writer may request in writing that such Composition revert to the Writer.

**5.2 Override Blocking Reversion and Direct MCSK Writer's Share Collection.** **Notwithstanding Clause 3.2 or Clause 5.1**, no Composition (whether exploited or unexploited) shall ever revert to the Writer unless and until the Writer has repaid to the Publisher in cash one hundred percent (100%) of the KES 150,000 Advance plus compound interest at eighteen percent (18%) per annum; and the Writer hereby grants the Publisher an irrevocable Power of Attorney to collect one hundred percent (100%) of the **Writer's Share** directly from the Music Copyright Society of Kenya (MCSK) until such sums are repaid.
